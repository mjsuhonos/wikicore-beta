Why Wiki Core

Familiar practice, web-native foundations

hub

Anchored in Wikidata

Stable Wikidata QIDs separate meaning from syntax and tie every concept into the wider linked-data ecosystem.

account_tree

Controlled vocabulary, not ontology

Practical SKOS concept schemes for metadata, indexing, and classification — non-prescriptive by design.

menu_book

Paired full-text corpus

Each concept links to an English Wikipedia article — a readable text and a training document in one.

language

Multilingual

Native labels in seven languages: en, ca, de, es, fi, fr, it.

extension

Modular subject groups

61 reusable vocabularies across core, class, and occupation groups — free to combine, extend, and overlap.

volunteer_activism

Humans in control

Automation handles extraction and linking; people define concepts, scope, and relationships.

Two sources, one system

Wikidata + Wikipedia

dns

Wikidata

Stable identifiers, multilingual labels, and structured relationships — subclass of, part of, instance of, occupation.

article

Wikipedia

Article text and the links between concepts, processed into clean JSONL documents keyed by QID.

Two guarantees: every entry links to an English Wikipedia article — and every entry has an English training document for automated indexing.

Built for automated indexing

From public dumps to subject indexing

1

filter_altExtract

Wikidata properties (P31, P279, P361, P106) and sitelinks; Wikipedia XML converted to clean JSONL by wiki2jsonl.py.

2

constructionBuild

SKOS vocabularies (.nt / .ttl.gz) and full-text corpora, split 80/10/10 into train, eval, and test sets.

3

rocket_launchIndex

Load into Annif to train, evaluate, and run automated subject indexing.

One command, make all, reproduces the release from source dumps.
